Privacy Policy

Tracers: One Line Puzzle · Last updated 13 November 2026

Tracers is a puzzle game where you draw one line through every cell of a grid. This page explains what information is used when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. Your progress, streak and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no servers of its own: levels and the daily puzzle are built into the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, including rewarded ads you can choose to watch for hints or to skip a level. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, internet address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Through AdMob, some ads are provided by Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms). When such an ad is shown, Meta may collect and use similar information, such as your device's advertising ID, internet address, approximate location derived from it, device and app details, and how you interact with ads, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. This is described in Meta's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings, under "Ad privacy settings". On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact details shown on the app's Google Play listing.
